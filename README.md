## 理论背景 / Background

FRT（Factor Recursion Theory，因子递归理论）不依赖数学方程，而是基于自然规律（热力学 / 时空连续性）与工程哲学发展而来。其核心突破是：将传统的平面/线性二进制计算机语言，重构为携带时间、空间、温度、位置、物理尺度与因果链条的立体多维语言。

FRT does not rely on mathematical equations; it is developed from natural laws (thermodynamics / spacetime continuity) and engineering philosophy. Its core breakthrough is restructuring flat, linear binary computer language into a multidimensional language that carries time, space, temperature, position, physical scale, and causal chains.

- 多维语言重构：每一条数字指令（文本、代码或信号）都被强制锚定在时间、空间、温度、位置及因果链五个物理维度上。
- Multidimensional language restructuring: every digital instruction (text, code, or signal) is anchored to five physical dimensions — time, space, temperature, position, and causal chain.
- 因果闭环约束：每一个操作的触发必须满足因果闭合性；不符合物理因果链或时空逻辑的操作，在底层无法通过物理验证，直接被拦截或触发自毁。
- Causally closed constraints: every operation must satisfy causal closure; operations violating physical causal chains or spacetime logic fail physical verification and are intercepted or self-destructed.
